# Surface: Cellar app (lists, bottle, add)

Mode: Operate. Platform: web (iPhone PWA, standalone, portrait). Follows the phone's light/dark setting.
Chosen decision sketch (critique reference, code-led build, no comp): `.impeccable/mocks/decision/vc-tonight.html`
(Vintage Chart · Tonight). Owner steer: "smoother with nicer fonts and arrangement"; "I do want the bottle image to be shown".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The cellar is a vintage chart, not a card feed. Every bottle is one row whose drinking window is drawn as a ribbon on a shared year axis with a single "now" line, so "what do I open?" is answered by looking down one vertical line. Refuses the default stacked-card list with status pills.

OWN-WORLD: Cool mineral ground (#F4F6F8 / #101216), near-black ink as the only tint, stage colours carry all expression: slate hold, sage ready, gold peak (thicker, glowing pill), dusty rose past. Bricolage Grotesque (optical sizes) everywhere, tabular year figures. Bottle photos as small portrait tiles on white.

STORY: The owner opens the app and sees one bottle recommended for tonight with the reason, then every other bottle grouped by urgency (past, drink soon, at its peak, ready, hold), and taps through to log a tasting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large "Cellar" title + bottle count; "Open tonight" card (eyebrow, status chip, bottle photo, 28px name, meta, big ribbon with Peak and Now ticks, one-sentence advice); search; In the cellar / Finished segment; filter row; first section header with axis labels. Scan camera button centred in the translucent tab bar, "Manually" under it.

FORM: Vintage Chart (model pick, round 1), variant Tonight. seed: c1df65abfc508312

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
